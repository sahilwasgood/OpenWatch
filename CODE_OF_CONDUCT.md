# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Behave like a guest in someone else's repository: assume good faith, disagree with the change
rather than the person, and keep the discussion technical.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or slurs.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive arguments or sustained derailment.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests and all other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or
remove content and may ban accounts for repeated or serious violations. Report problems to the
maintainer; reports are handled privately and the maintainer's decision on enforcement is final.
